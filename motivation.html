Our Purpose

Closing the Loop on Food Waste.

We are using emerging technology to revive the sustainable kitchen.

The Problem

Households waste nearly 30% of the food they buy. This isn't just money down the drain—it contributes massively to global greenhouse gas emissions. The problem isn't that people don't care; it's that tracking ingredients manually is tedious and difficult.

The Eco Plate Solution

Eco Plate is a web-based application designed to bridge the gap between your fridge and your dinner table. By integrating Smart Scan Technology (Computer Vision), we remove the friction of manual tracking.

  • Automated Inventory: No more typing. The camera logs what you buy.
  • Zero-Waste Recipes: Our algorithm prioritizes ingredients that are about to expire.
  • Cost Savings: Eat what you have, save money on groceries.
