Eco Plate — Motivation

Description

Eco Plate is a mobile-based application that helps users cook more sustainably by reducing food waste and saving money. Users input the ingredients they already have at home and receive recipe suggestions that are low-waste, seasonal, and cost-effective. Much like apps such as Yummly or Allrecipes, this app focuses on sustainability, guiding users toward efficient ingredient use and minimizing food waste.

Web-App Features

  • Ingredient input form to track what users have at home.
  • Recipe suggestions based on ingredients, sustainability, and seasonality.
  • Integration with a reliable recipe database via API.
  • Personalized recommendations to reduce waste and save money.
  • Progress tracking and tips for environmentally conscious cooking habits.
  • Mobile-friendly interface for easy use anywhere.

Web-App Operation

Users land on the home screen and enter the ingredients they currently have. They click “Get Recipes” to see a curated list of sustainable, low-waste recipes. Each recipe displays used ingredients, missing ingredients, and a link to view the full instructions. Users can scroll through results, click on recipes they like, and start cooking while following the app’s guidance on efficient ingredient use. Through repeated sessions, the app tracks usage patterns and provides feedback on reducing food waste and saving money.
